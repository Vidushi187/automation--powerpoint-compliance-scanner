# Automated PowerPoint Independence Compliance Scanner

## Overview
An automated pre-issuance quality assurance scanner embedded within Microsoft PowerPoint to identify, flag, and visually highlight non-compliant terminology across client-facing deliverables and pitch decks[cite: 1, 2]. Built to enforce firm and regulatory auditor independence rules (including SEC, PCAOB, and CPAB standards), the tool replaces manual, slide-by-slide checks with an in-situ review mechanism that accelerates turnaround and reduces regulatory risk prior to external distribution[cite: 1, 2].

### Key Impact & Metrics
* **Detection Accuracy:** Targets >= 98% detection of defined restricted phrases and independence risk terms[cite: 1, 2].
* **Review Efficiency:** Reduces deck proofing turnaround from 45–60 minutes down to < 30 seconds for a 50-slide presentation[cite: 1, 2].
* **Compliance Gate:** Aims for a >= 95% first-time pass rate on initial Quality & Risk Management (QRM) submissions[cite: 1, 2].
* **Escalation Reduction:** Targets a 75% drop in avoidable terminology-related escalations to the National Independence Office[cite: 1, 2].
